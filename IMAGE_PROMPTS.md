# Image prompts for Our Table (ChatGPT image generation)

Sizes ChatGPT can make: **landscape 1536x1024**, **portrait 1024x1536**, square 1024x1024.
Plan: 9 generations in total (1A, 1B, 1C, 1D, 2A, 2B, 7A, 7B, plus the optional 7 mini sheet). If you can only make a few, do 1A, 2A, 7A and 7B first: they change the look the most. Save each result under the file name given, in the app folder `assets/incoming/`, and tell me — I cut the sheets into pieces, remove backgrounds, and wire everything into the games.

**House style (put this at the start of every prompt, or say "same style as before"):**
> Cinematic, richly detailed, painterly-photorealistic digital art. Deep emerald-green and warm brass-gold colour palette, warm lamplight and soft rim light, subtle film grain, luxurious "private members' club" mood. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks, no people's faces.

**Rules for the sheets (important for cutting them up):** every cell is separated by a clear empty gutter, all cells are the same size, every object is fully inside its cell and never touches the edge, flat unlit background inside the cells, no captions.
