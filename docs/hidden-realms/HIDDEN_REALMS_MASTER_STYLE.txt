THE HIDDEN REALMS - MASTER STYLE (short version for image prompts)
Based on docs/Fantasy Design Concept Prompt.pdf, adjusted on 2026-11-20 so that it does NOT look like the emerald-and-brass OUR TABLE club identity, and so that it follows its own rule "magic is discovered, not advertised".

HOW TO USE: paste this block at the START of any ChatGPT image prompt for a Hidden Realms asset, then add the specific asset description (and, for games, the technical rules: aspect ratio, empty background, no text, transparency).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE BLOCK (paste this)

World: THE HIDDEN REALMS, an ancient society of explorers and scholars who discover, document and protect forgotten places, creatures and objects. Its headquarters has a vast candlelit library, a natural-history museum, an observatory, alchemical workshops and deep vaults. Every object looks as if it has a history and a purpose.
Look: cinematic painterly realism, premium fantasy-film production quality, hand-crafted and aged. Not cartoon, not childish, not neon, not over-saturated, not generic mobile-game art, no science-fiction, no modern technology.
Main colours: midnight blue, deep burgundy, aged parchment, weathered stone, aged silver. Accent: antique gold and warm bronze (gold looks like real metal with soft reflections, never flat yellow). Emerald green only as a small accent, never as the main colour.
Materials: hand-carved dark walnut and oak, embossed leather, engraved brass, bronze and silver, hand-blown glass, dark polished stone, weathered parchment, old book bindings, wax seals, fine botanical engravings.
Light: warm candlelight, soft light through tall Gothic windows, controlled shadows, gentle reflections on metal and glass. Mysterious and inviting, never frightening, never too dark: important game elements must stay clearly readable.
Magic: subtle and rare. Nothing glows unless the asset description says so. No floating particles, no magical smoke by default.
Ornament language (reuse everywhere): engraved borders, small crests and wax seals, celestial diagrams, old map details, antique keys, discreet runes, botanical sprigs (oak, laurel, fern).
Architecture: Victorian, Gothic, medieval and classical European: vaulted ceilings, arched doorways, carved panels, brass railings, tall windows.
Never: text or letters in the picture (unless asked), logos, copyrighted characters, props or locations from existing films, books or games.
Consistency rule: every asset must look made by the same studio for the same universe: same colours, materials, light and level of detail.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE FIVE REALMS (each can serve several games)
1 The Grand Archive: libraries, manuscripts, globes, old maps (quiz, word, puzzle games)
2 The Cabinet of Curiosities: glass cases, rare objects, instruments (memory, matching, collecting)
3 The Enchanted Wilds: ancient trees, ferns, misty paths. Keep it natural and painterly with at most small amber lights, NOT the neon glowing mushroom forests of the mood pictures (adventure, reaction)
4 The Celestial Observatory: astrolabes, star charts, orreries, brass instruments (dice, chance, prediction)
5 The Forgotten Vaults: stone vault doors, rune carvings, antique gold, locked chambers (competition, rewards)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXTRA RULES (added 2026-11-20 after a review of the master style; the style block above stays exactly as it is, to keep 50 or 100 assets consistent)
1 Age and authenticity: nothing looks newly built. Craftsmanship, wear, repairs and patina; books look handled, instruments look usable, rooms look lived in.
2 Each realm has its own dominant character:
   Grand Archive: parchment, walnut, burgundy, warm candlelight
   Cabinet of Curiosities: dark wood, glass, aged silver, bronze
   Enchanted Wilds: natural greens, moss, earth, mist, muted daylight
   Celestial Observatory: midnight blue, silver, dark stone, antique brass
   Forgotten Vaults: weathered stone, dark iron, aged gold, dramatic shadows
3 Environment art and game interface are separate: backgrounds give atmosphere and never compete with gameplay; boards, cards, scoreboards and controls get their own clear design with the same materials and ornament language.
THE TEST for every new asset: MAGIC IS DISCOVERED, NOT ADVERTISED. If it looks like generic magical fantasy artwork it is wrong; if it looks like a believable historical place or object that hides something extraordinary, it belongs.
